CareConnect – A Unified Healthcare Communication Platform
"CareConnect" is an enterprise-grade care coordination and patient communication platform widely used by hospitals and large healthcare networks. As part of its 2025 product roadmap, multiple new feature requests have been gathered from various stakeholders — physicians, nurses, IT administrators, patient advocates, and external compliance teams. These features aim to improve clinical communication, workflow automation, and patient engagement.

Features:

Secure Image Sharing: Allow clinicians to securely share images (X-rays, photos) in patient threads.
EMR Integration Enhancements: Improve real-time syncing of patient status with EMR systems.
Auto-Translation in Chat: Automatically translate patient messages into provider's preferred language.
Care Pathway Templates: Predefined care communication templates by condition (e.g., diabetes, asthma).
User Access Audit Logs: Detailed logs of who accessed which patient data, addressing compliance concerns
Patient Mood Tracker: Weekly patient-reported mood check-in and graph trends over time.
