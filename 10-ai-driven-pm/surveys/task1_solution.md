# Business Analyst Onboarding Challenges Survey

---

## About the Survey

Thank you for taking the time to participate in this survey. As our account continues to grow rapidly, we are committed to improving the onboarding experience for new projects. The purpose of this survey is to **identify the types, frequency, and complexity of challenges** that Business Analysts face during the onboarding process for new projects.

Your responses will help us understand common pain points, pinpoint the stages where issues most frequently arise, and prioritize improvements to make onboarding smoother and more efficient for everyone.

The survey contains **13 questions** and should take approximately **5–7 minutes** to complete. All responses will be used solely for internal process improvement purposes.

If you have any questions about this survey, please contact the Account Management team.
