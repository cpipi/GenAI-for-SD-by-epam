Context
Your account is expected to grow quickly, but you have noted challenges and complexities in the onboarding process. Your goal is to quickly collect general information about these challenges. You should create and conduct a survey to identify the types, frequency, and complexity of challenges experienced by business analysts during the onboarding of new projects.

Task
Create a survey using EPAM DIAL (choose any LLM you want). The survey should include at least 7 questions. Most of the questions must be closed-ended, with multiple-choice answers. Focus the questions on identifying the main challenges, their complexity, and the typical stages at which these issues occur during the onboarding process.

Additionally, consider gathering details about the respondents (seniority level, location, current project role) for more detailed insights. Remember to include a brief ‘About the Survey’ section at the beginning. Please note that the survey will be distributed via email.

Provide the generated survey as the solution for the task. List of questions with options has to be formatted in MD formatting.
