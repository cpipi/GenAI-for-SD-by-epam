Task 2. Creating technical documentation.
Once your AI Agent is set up, follow these steps to complete the reverse engineering task:

Step 1: Initiate a Chat with Your AI Agent

Open the interface of your chosen AI platform and start a conversation with your AI agent.
Step 2: Generate Documentation

Request the AI Agent to generate the Technical documentation (e.g., Low-Level Design, architecture diagrams) in a Markdown format.
Step 4: Review and Copy the Output

Review the documentation generated by the AI agent.

Ask the AI agent to make any adjustments, if needed.

Copy the output.

Step 5: Submit Your Solution

Submit the generated documentation as your solution for the task.
